Cafe wait times compared by mean and median

Write code · Medium · §8.1 Differences the average hides

Lunchtime order wait times (minutes) at two campus cafes, East and West, were measured six times each and stored in the DataFrame waits. Make two Axes in 1 row and 2 columns that share the y-axis, and draw bar charts of the mean for each cafe on the left Axes and the median for each cafe on the right Axes.
