Write code · Challenge · §6.3 Axes, ticks, and units

Draw the weekly follower counts of the university's main social media account (campus) and a newly opened festival account (festival) as two lines on one Axes. Draw campus first and festival next, label them Campus and Festival, and show the legend.

To compare how many times larger the counts became, change the y-axis to a log scale.
