Wait time densities for two rides with different numbers of records

Write code · Medium · §8.3 Comparing groups by density

These are wait times (minutes) measured at two rides in an amusement park. The roller coaster (coaster) was recorded 10 times and the carousel (carousel) 5 times, so it is hard to compare the shapes using counts. Make two Axes in 1 row and 2 columns that share the y-axis, and draw the histograms of the two rides as densities with the same bin edges, edges. coaster goes on the left and carousel on the right.

A density does not show the number of observations, so put it in the titles, like Coaster (n=10) and Carousel (n=5). The axis labels that state the units (Wait (min), Density (1/min)) are not graded. Grading redraws the charts with different values in coaster and carousel.
