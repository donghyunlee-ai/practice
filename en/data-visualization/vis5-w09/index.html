A heatmap of averages by OS and age group

Write code · Challenge · §5.5 Reviewing and reading your chart choice

To check the claim "Android users in their teens use apps the longest," split the daily app usage time (minutes) of 12 respondents by two criteria, operating system (os) and age group (age), and look at the averages.
