A day's records on 2×2 Axes

Fill in the blanks · Basic · §3.2 Arranging and reaching Axes

Draw four kinds of hourly library records on Axes in 2 rows and 2 columns. Fill in the three blanks so that, starting at the top left and going along each row, the charts appear in this order: loans as a line, returns as a line, visitors as bars, and seats (seats used) as bars.
