Charts for distribution and relationship questions

Fill in the blanks · Basic · §1.1 Visualization answers questions

Draw two charts side by side that answer two questions. The left chart (ax1) shows how daily steps over 20 days are spread out, and the right chart (ax2) shows whether the dorm uses more power on days with a higher daytime high.

In each of the two blanks, write the drawing command that fits the question. Draw a histogram with hist(list of values) and a scatter plot with scatter(x values, y values). On the scatter plot, put the temperature temp on the x-axis and the power use power on the y-axis.
