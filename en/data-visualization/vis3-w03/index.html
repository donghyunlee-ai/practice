Four charts for four questions

Write code · Medium · §3.2 Arranging and reaching Axes

Draw a university's application data by department as four different charts on Axes in 2 rows and 2 columns. For each one, choose the plotting method that fits the question.

At the top left, draw a horizontal bar chart of applicants to compare the number of applicants by department. The department names in depts are long, so horizontal bars are easier to read. Keep the bars in the order barh() draws them: pass depts as it is, without reversing it or flipping the y-axis. The title is Applicants.

At the top right, draw a pie chart of each department's share of all applicants, and label each wedge with the department name. The title is Share.

At the bottom left, draw the distribution of interview scores, scores, as a histogram with 5 bins. The title is Interview scores.

At the bottom right, draw a scatter plot with the admission quota, quota, on the x-axis and the number of applicants, applicants, on the y-axis. The title is Quota vs applicants.
